{# The last thing in the head of every sign-in screen. A press of the main button answers at once. Signing in takes the server a moment - the password is checked with a deliberately slow hash - and a button that does nothing for that moment gets pressed again: which posts the form twice, and looks broken. It now says what it is doing, and a second press waits for the first. The files the next page needs - the app's stylesheet, its navigation layer, the icon font - are fetched once this page has finished, not alongside it. Asked for in the head, they shared the line with this page's own typefaces and logo and slowed them down; afterwards they still arrive long before anybody has typed a password. #}
