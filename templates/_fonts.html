{# The app's two typefaces - Manrope to read, Fraunces for headlines - served from this site rather than from Google Fonts. From Google they cost a DNS lookup and a TLS handshake to two more hosts before a letter could change, most of a second on a phone's mobile data, and they told Google about every visit. From here they come over the connection the page already has, stamped and cached for a year. Each is a variable font, so one file carries every weight; latin-ext is only fetched for a character that needs it (the rupee sign is one). Text draws at once in the fallback face and swaps over. The reading face is preloaded: it is on every page from the first line. The headline face is fetched when a headline asks for it. #}
